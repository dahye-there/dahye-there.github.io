About

Families, Relationships, and Aging Across the Life Course.

My research focuses on family demography, family sociology, and aging, with a particular emphasis on East Asia. I study intergenerational relationships, gender, inheritance, childlessness, and widowhood, primarily using quantitative methods and large-scale survey data.

My work has been published in journals including the Journal of Marriage and Family, Social Science & Medicine, The Journals of Gerontology: Series B, Population, and Research on Aging. My current research examines family support and vulnerability surrounding widowhood, inheritance and intergenerational support, and aging without children across East Asian and comparative contexts.

I am currently an Assistant Professor of Sociology at Hong Kong Baptist University. I received my Ph.D. in Public Policy from the Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy at the National University of Singapore in 2022. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Centre for Family and Population Research at NUS and the Department of Social Work and Social Administration at the University of Hong Kong. I hold master's and bachelor's degrees in Public Administration from Korea University.
